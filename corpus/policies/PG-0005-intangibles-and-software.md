# Intangible Assets and Internally Developed Software

Software is where capitalisation policy is most often misapplied, because the spend looks like
payroll and the benefit looks like an asset. This policy sets out when development cost becomes an
asset and when it does not.

## 1. Research and development

Research expenditure is expensed as incurred. There is no exception to this and no ability to
capitalise retrospectively once a project later succeeds.

Development expenditure is capitalised only when **all** of the following are demonstrated and
documented before the spend is incurred:

1. the project is technically feasible;
2. management intends to complete it and use or sell the output;
3. the Group is able to use or sell the output;
4. the output will generate probable future economic benefit, evidenced by a business case;
5. adequate technical, financial and other resources are available to complete it;
6. the expenditure attributable to the project can be measured reliably.

Failure of any one criterion at any point stops capitalisation from that date forward. Amounts
already capitalised are then tested for impairment under PG-0030.

## 2. Threshold

The **software capitalisation threshold of USD 10,000** applies to total qualifying development
cost for a project, not to individual invoices or timesheet lines. A project expected to fall
below the threshold is expensed from the outset rather than accumulated and reassessed.

## 3. Project phases

| Phase | Typical activities | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Preliminary | Scoping, vendor selection, proof of concept, feasibility | Expense |
| Development | Design, coding, configuration, testing, data conversion routines | Capitalise if criteria in §1 met |
| Post-implementation | Training, data migration execution, maintenance, minor enhancement | Expense |

Costs that may be capitalised in the development phase are directly attributable: internal
developer time at actual cost, external contractor cost, and licence cost for tools used solely to
build the asset. General management time, recruitment, and allocated overhead may not be
capitalised. Internal time is capitalised only where it is supported by timesheet records
identifying the project; a percentage estimate applied to a team is not acceptable evidence.

## 4. Cloud and subscription arrangements

Where the Group does not obtain control of the software — the typical software-as-a-service
arrangement — the subscription fee is an operating expense over the service period. Configuration
and customisation costs in such arrangements are expensed unless they create a separately
identifiable asset the Group controls, which is uncommon and requires a written technical position
from Group Technical Accounting before any amount is capitalised.

Implementation costs paid to a third party for a cloud arrangement are expensed as the service is
received. Prepaid multi-year subscriptions are held as prepayments and released over the term.

## 5. Amortisation

Capitalised software is amortised on a straight-line basis over its expected useful life, normally
three to five years, beginning when the asset is ready for its intended use. Useful lives longer
than five years require Finance Director approval and a documented basis. The Group does not
assume indefinite useful lives for software.

## 6. Other intangibles

| Class | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Purchased licences and perpetual software | Capitalised at cost; amortised over the shorter of licence term and five years |
| Customer relationships acquired in a business combination | Recognised at fair value; amortised over expected relationship life |
| Brands and trade names acquired | Recognised at fair value; indefinite life only where supportable and tested annually |
| Internally generated brands, customer lists and goodwill | Never recognised |
| Website development | Content and graphic design expensed; underlying platform assessed under §1 |

## 7. Goodwill

Goodwill arising on acquisition is not amortised. It is allocated to cash-generating units and
tested for impairment annually and whenever an indicator arises, under PG-0030. Goodwill is never
written back once impaired.
