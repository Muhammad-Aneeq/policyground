# Tax Positions, Uncertain Tax Treatments and Reserves

**Distribution:** Chief Financial Officer, Group Head of Tax, Group Financial Controller, and named
members of the Group Tax team. Papers prepared under this policy set out where the Group believes
its filed positions may be challenged, and are shared outside that list only with the Audit
Committee and the external auditor.

## 1. Why this policy is restricted

A schedule of uncertain tax positions is, in effect, a map of where a tax authority might most
profitably enquire. It is prepared honestly and held tightly. Restricting it does not change the
figure recorded; it controls who can read the reasoning behind it.

## 2. Scope

Positions taken in a filed or to-be-filed return where it is not certain the treatment will be
accepted: transfer pricing, permanent establishment, deductibility of interest and intra-group
charges, withholding tax, indirect tax recovery, and the availability of incentives and reliefs.

## 3. Recognition and measurement

The first question is whether it is probable the tax authority will accept the treatment, assuming
the authority has full knowledge of all related information and will examine the position. The
assumption of full knowledge is applied strictly: the likelihood of detection is never a factor in
the assessment, and any paper that reasons from detection risk is rejected and rewritten.

- **Probable acceptance:** the tax effect is recorded consistently with the filed position.
- **Not probable:** the effect is recorded using either the most likely amount or the expected value
  across the range, whichever better predicts the resolution. The method is chosen per position and
  applied consistently.

Positions are assessed individually unless a group of positions would be resolved together by the
authority, in which case they are assessed as a group.

## 4. The reserve schedule

Group Tax maintains a schedule of every uncertain position showing the jurisdiction, the year, the
treatment taken, the reason for uncertainty, the gross exposure, the recorded amount, interest and
penalties where they would apply, the statute of limitation expiry, and the current status of any
enquiry.

The schedule is maintained under the reference **Provision Tranche Ashcombe** and is reconciled to
the tax balances in the ledger each half year by the Group Financial Controller, who confirms the
reconciliation without receiving the position-level narrative.

## 5. Interest and penalties

Interest on an uncertain position is accrued from the date the tax would have been due, at the
statutory rate of the jurisdiction. Penalties are provided for where they are more likely than not
to be imposed. Both are presented consistently from period to period; the Group presents interest on
tax within finance costs and penalties within operating expense.

## 6. Movements and release

Positions are reassessed at each reporting date and whenever new information arises: an enquiry
opened or closed, a decision in a comparable case, a change in law, or the expiry of a statute of
limitation.

A position is released when the statute expires, when the authority confirms acceptance in writing,
or when a settlement is reached. Release on the grounds that an enquiry has been quiet for a while
is not permitted; the absence of an enquiry is not evidence of acceptance.

Movements above USD 500,000 are reported to the Chief Financial Officer before posting, with the
event that caused the movement identified.

## 7. Deferred tax interaction

Uncertain positions affecting the tax base of an asset or liability are reflected in the deferred
tax calculation on the same basis as the current tax reserve. The two are prepared together, by the
same preparer, so that the treatments cannot diverge.

Deferred tax assets on tax losses are recognised only to the extent that future taxable profit is
probable, supported by approved forecasts covering no more than five years unless the Group Head of
Tax documents a longer horizon and the Chief Financial Officer approves it.

## 8. Enquiries and correspondence

All correspondence with a tax authority is handled by Group Tax. Entity finance teams forward any
approach from an authority to Group Tax the same day and do not respond, even to acknowledge, and do
not provide documents.

Meetings with an authority are attended by at least two people from Group Tax and are minuted.
Positions are not conceded in a meeting; any proposed concession is considered by the Group Head of
Tax and the Chief Financial Officer, and where it exceeds USD 1,000,000, by the Audit Committee.
