# Delegation of Authority and Approval Matrix

This policy states who may commit the Group to expenditure, and to what value. It is the reference
point cited by every other policy in this manual that mentions an approval.

## 1. Scope

The matrix in §3 applies to any commitment of Group funds: purchase orders, contracts, expense
claims, capital projects, settlements, write-offs, and any arrangement that creates an obligation
whether or not an invoice has been received.

Authority is personal to the role holder. It may be delegated temporarily during absence, in
writing, to a named individual at no more than the delegator's own level, for a stated period not
exceeding sixty days.

## 2. How the bands work

Bands are applied to the **total value of the commitment over its life**, not to the annual value
and not to the value of a single instalment. A three-year contract at USD 40,000 per year is a
USD 120,000 commitment and requires approval at that level.

Splitting a commitment to bring it within a lower band is a serious control breach. It is reported
to the Financial Controller and to Internal Audit, and it is treated as a disciplinary matter
irrespective of whether the underlying spend was legitimate.

## 3. Approval matrix

| Band | Approver | Limit (total commitment value) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost Centre Manager | Up to USD 5,000 |
| 2 | Department Head | Up to USD 25,000 |
| 3 | Finance Director | Up to USD 100,000 |
| 4 | Chief Financial Officer | Up to USD 500,000 |
| 5 | Chief Executive Officer | Up to USD 2,000,000 |
| 6 | Board of Directors | Above USD 2,000,000 |

Stated in full:

- A **Cost Centre Manager may approve commitments up to USD 5,000**.
- A **Department Head may approve commitments up to USD 25,000**.
- The **Finance Director may approve commitments up to USD 100,000**.
- The **Chief Financial Officer may approve commitments up to USD 500,000**.
- The **Chief Executive Officer may approve commitments up to USD 2,000,000**.
- Any commitment above USD 2,000,000 requires Board approval. There is no individual authority
  above Band 5.

Approval at a band includes the authority of every band below it. Approval is sequential where a
second signature is required by §5, not parallel.

## 4. Matters reserved regardless of value

The following require the stated approval however small the amount:

| Matter | Approver |
|---|---|
| Any commitment to a related party | Chief Financial Officer and Audit Committee |
| Any settlement of a legal claim | Chief Financial Officer and Group Counsel |
| Any guarantee, indemnity or letter of comfort | Board of Directors |
| Any new bank account or change of bank mandate | Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer |
| Any write-off of a receivable | Finance Director, per PG-0028 |
| Any exception to a capitalisation threshold, including the **general capitalisation threshold of USD 5,000** | Finance Director, per PG-0003 |
| Any disposal or write-off of an asset with a **net book value above USD 50,000** | Finance Director, per PG-0004 |
| Any change to a person's pay, other than through the annual review cycle | Chief Financial Officer and Chief People Officer |

## 5. Second signature

A second, independent signature at the next band up is required where:

- the supplier is new and has not completed onboarding under PG-0008;
- the commitment is a sole-source award above USD 50,000;
- payment terms depart from the Group standard;
- the commitment arises from an emergency and is being ratified after the event.

## 6. Segregation of duties

The person who raises a requisition may not approve it. The person who approves a purchase order
may not release the payment. The person who maintains supplier master data may not approve
payments to suppliers. Where headcount makes full segregation impossible in a small entity, the
compensating control is documented, approved by the Financial Controller, and reviewed annually.

## 7. Emergency authority

Where a commitment cannot wait for normal approval — a safety issue, a regulatory deadline, or a
business-continuity event — the Finance Director may authorise up to USD 250,000 with subsequent
ratification at the correct band within five working days. Emergency authority is not available for
recurring spend, and a second use for the same underlying issue is not an emergency.

## 8. Reporting

A quarterly report of all approvals at Band 4 and above, and of every use of emergency authority,
goes to the Audit Committee. Breaches of this policy are reported at the next meeting after
discovery, not aggregated to year end.
