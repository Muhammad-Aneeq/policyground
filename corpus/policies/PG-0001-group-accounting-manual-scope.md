# Group Accounting Manual — Scope, Authority and Governance

This policy establishes what the Calder Meridian Group Accounting Manual is, who it binds, and
how it is changed. Every other policy in this manual is subordinate to it. Where a local
statutory requirement conflicts with this manual, the local requirement prevails for statutory
reporting only, and the difference is recorded in the local-to-group reconciliation.

## 1. Purpose

The manual exists so that the same transaction is accounted for the same way in every entity of
the Group. It is not a training document and it does not restate accounting standards. It records
the Group's chosen treatment where a standard permits a choice, and the Group's chosen thresholds
where a standard is silent.

## 2. Scope and application

This manual applies to:

- every entity in which the Group holds a controlling interest, including entities acquired
  mid-year from the date control passes;
- every branch, representative office and dormant entity carried in the consolidation;
- shared-service centres preparing records on behalf of a Group entity.

It does not apply to joint ventures and associates accounted for under the equity method, other
than to the extent needed to align their reported results for equity accounting.

## 3. Authority and hierarchy

Where guidance conflicts, the following order applies, highest first:

1. Applicable accounting standards and law.
2. A written technical position issued by Group Technical Accounting for a specific transaction.
3. This manual.
4. Local finance procedures and desk instructions.

No local procedure may set a threshold lower or higher than a threshold set in this manual. A
local team that believes a Group threshold is inappropriate for its market raises an exception
request rather than departing from the manual quietly.

## 4. Materiality

The Group applies a **group performance materiality of USD 1,500,000** for consolidated reporting
purposes. Component materiality is allocated by Group Technical Accounting each year and
communicated to each reporting unit before the interim close.

Individually small misstatements are not ignored merely because they are small. Items below the
**clearly trivial threshold of USD 75,000** need not be accumulated on the summary of uncorrected
misstatements, but any misstatement arising from a control failure, a management override, or a
deliberate act is reported regardless of amount.

Materiality is a reporting concept. It is never a reason to bypass an approval limit, skip a
reconciliation, or leave a known error uncorrected in the underlying ledger.

## 5. Policy maintenance

Policies carry a version number and an effective date. Changes follow this route:

| Change type | Proposed by | Approved by | Notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial (no change in meaning) | Policy owner | Group Financial Controller | None |
| Threshold or treatment change | Policy owner | Finance Director | 30 days before effective date |
| New policy, or withdrawal of a policy | Group Technical Accounting | Chief Financial Officer | 60 days |

Superseded versions are retained for seven years. A policy without a named owner is treated as
withdrawn at its next review date.

## 6. Interpretation requests

Where this manual does not address a transaction, the reporting unit does not improvise. It
submits an interpretation request to Group Technical Accounting with the contract, the amounts,
and the treatment it proposes. Responses are published as technical positions and, where they
recur, folded into this manual at the next revision.
