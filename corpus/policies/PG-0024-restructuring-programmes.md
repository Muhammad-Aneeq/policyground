# Restructuring Programmes and Exceptional Item Presentation

**Distribution:** Chief Financial Officer, Finance Director, Group Financial Controller, Group
Counsel, Chief People Officer, and named programme finance leads. A restructuring plan is
market-sensitive and affects identifiable employees before it is announced; access is controlled for
both reasons.

## 1. Why this policy is restricted

Recognition depends on whether a plan has been communicated. Circulating the plan to establish the
accounting would itself be the communication, which would trigger recognition and pre-empt the
decision. The order matters, so the audience is narrow.

## 2. When a restructuring provision is recognised

A provision is recognised only when the Group has a detailed formal plan **and** has raised a valid
expectation in those affected that it will carry the plan out, by starting to implement it or by
announcing its main features to those affected.

A detailed formal plan identifies at minimum: the business or part of the business concerned, the
principal locations affected, the location, function and approximate number of employees who will be
compensated for terminating their services, the expenditure that will be undertaken, and when the
plan will be implemented.

Board approval alone does not create an obligation. Nor does a management decision, a leaked
document, or a plan communicated so vaguely that no individual could form an expectation. The date
of communication is evidenced and retained, because it determines the period of recognition.

## 3. What may be included

Only direct expenditures arising necessarily from the restructuring and not associated with the
ongoing activities of the Group:

| Included | Excluded |
|---|---|
| Termination benefits for roles being eliminated | Retraining or relocation of continuing staff |
| Contract termination penalties | Marketing or investment in new systems |
| Onerous contract charges arising from the plan | Costs of integrating a retained business |
| Site closure and make-good obligations | Future operating losses of the continuing business |
| Programme-specific external adviser cost | Costs of employees who will continue in a different role |

Future operating losses are never provided for. A provision that includes them is corrected in the
period identified, with the correction disclosed.

## 4. Programme identification

Each programme is given a reference at approval and every cost, provision movement and utilisation
is tagged with it, so that the total programme cost is traceable and the charge cannot drift into
underlying results. The current group-wide efficiency programme is referenced as **Programme
Silverbank** and all associated purchase orders carry that reference in the project segment of the
coding block under PG-0002.

## 5. Onerous contracts

A contract is onerous where the unavoidable cost of meeting its obligations exceeds the economic
benefit expected from it. The unavoidable cost is the lower of the cost of fulfilling and any
compensation payable on failing to fulfil.

Before providing for an onerous contract, any asset dedicated to that contract is tested for
impairment first under PG-0030, and impaired if necessary. Only the excess remaining is provided
for, so that the same loss is not recognised twice.

## 6. Utilisation and monitoring

Provisions are utilised only against the specific costs for which they were recognised, per PG-0023
§6. Programme finance leads report monthly: opening provision, additions, utilisation by category,
release, closing provision, and cash spent against provision.

Where utilisation is significantly slower than plan, the provision is reassessed rather than
retained on the basis that the money will eventually be spent. Unused amounts are released in the
period the plan changes, and the release is presented in the same line as the original charge.

## 7. Exceptional item presentation

Restructuring costs are presented separately from underlying results only where the programme is
approved under this policy, is genuinely non-recurring in character, and is disclosed consistently
from one period to the next.

Two disciplines apply and are enforced by the Group Financial Controller:

- **Symmetry.** If a cost was presented as exceptional, a later release of the same provision is
  also presented as exceptional. Charges are not exceptional while credits are underlying.
- **Consistency.** A category treated as underlying in one period is not reclassified as exceptional
  in a worse period. Any change of classification requires Audit Committee approval and is disclosed
  with the prior period restated for comparability.

Programmes running for more than three consecutive years are reviewed by the Audit Committee for
whether their costs remain genuinely exceptional in character.
