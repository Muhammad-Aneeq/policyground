# Intercompany Transactions, Recharges and Settlement

Intercompany balances eliminate on consolidation only if both sides agree. This policy sets out how
transactions between Group entities are initiated, agreed, settled and eliminated.

## 1. Principle

Every intercompany transaction has two sides posted in the **same period** at the **same amount** in
the transaction currency. An entity does not post its side and wait for the counterparty to catch
up; where agreement cannot be reached before the deadline in §4, neither side posts and the matter
is escalated.

## 2. Initiation

Intercompany charges are initiated by the providing entity, never by the receiving entity, and only
where a written intercompany agreement is in force. The agreement states the service, the basis of
charge, the currency and the frequency.

New recharge arrangements require Group Tax review before the first charge, because the basis of
charge has transfer pricing consequences that cannot be corrected retrospectively without
amendment. A recharge introduced mid-year without that review is reversed.

## 3. Categories and basis

| Category | Basis of charge | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Central function recharge | Agreed cost allocation key, set annually | Monthly |
| Shared service centre | Cost plus the agreed mark-up per the service agreement | Monthly |
| Goods transferred | Transfer price per the intercompany price list | On transfer |
| Seconded employee | Actual employment cost plus agreed uplift | Monthly |
| Funding and loans | Interest at the rate in the loan agreement | Monthly |
| Royalty and licence | Per the licence agreement | Quarterly |

Allocation keys are fixed at the start of the year and are not adjusted during the year to move
profit between entities. A change requires Group Tax and Finance Director approval.

## 4. Agreement and the close deadline

Counterparties agree balances by Working Day 4, ahead of **group submission on Working Day 8**. The
providing entity issues its statement by Working Day 2; the receiving entity confirms or disputes by
Working Day 4.

Silence is not agreement. An unconfirmed balance is treated as disputed and escalated to both
Financial Controllers on Working Day 5.

## 5. Tolerance

The **intercompany mismatch tolerance of USD 1,000** applies per counterparty pair per period.
Differences within tolerance are written off by the providing entity to the designated intercompany
difference account; differences above tolerance are resolved before submission, not carried forward.

Persistent differences within tolerance are not acceptable merely because each is small. Where the
same pair reports a difference in three consecutive periods, the cause is investigated regardless of
amount.

## 6. Settlement

Balances are settled in cash monthly through the Group netting cycle, unless the balance is a
formal loan documented under a loan agreement. Unsettled trading balances older than ninety days are
reported to the Treasurer and, absent an explanation, are converted to funding balances with
interest applied, because an interest-free long-term trading balance creates tax exposure.

Netting is operated centrally by Treasury. Entities do not settle bilaterally outside the netting
cycle without Treasurer approval.

## 7. Elimination

Group Reporting eliminates intercompany revenue and cost, balances, dividends and unrealised profit
in inventory and fixed assets on consolidation. Entities support elimination by coding the
counterparty entity in the intercompany segment of the coding block under PG-0002; an intercompany
transaction coded with counterparty `000` cannot be eliminated and is returned for correction.

Unrealised profit on inventory held within the Group at the period end is calculated by the holding
entity and reported with the submission.

## 8. Currency

Where the two entities have different functional currencies, the transaction is recorded by each in
its own functional currency at the rate specified in PG-0020. The resulting exchange difference is
real and is not eliminated on consolidation; it is reported separately so that it is not mistaken
for a reconciling difference.
