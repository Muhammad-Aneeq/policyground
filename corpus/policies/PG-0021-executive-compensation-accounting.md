# Executive Compensation, Retention and Severance Accounting

**Distribution:** Chief Financial Officer, Group Financial Controller, Group Reward Director, and
named individuals on the Executive Reward access list. This policy is not circulated to entity
finance teams. Where an entity must post an entry arising from it, the entry is prepared centrally
and issued as a top-side adjustment under PG-0016 §5.

## 1. Why this policy is restricted

Individual reward arrangements are personal data and are market-sensitive before announcement.
Accounting for them requires knowledge of the arrangement's terms, which cannot be circulated. The
accounting treatment itself is not secret; the inputs are.

## 2. Scope

Arrangements for members of the Executive Committee and for individuals designated as Material Risk
Takers: base salary, annual incentive, long-term incentive, retention awards, buy-out awards on
hire, and any severance or settlement on departure.

## 3. Annual incentive

Accrued over the performance period based on the outcome management expects at each reporting date,
not the target. Where the plan has a discretionary component, the accrual reflects the Committee's
expected exercise of that discretion based on its historical pattern, and the basis is documented
each quarter.

The accrual is revisited at each close. Where the expected outcome changes by more than 20 per cent
of the prior estimate, the Chief Financial Officer is notified before the entry is posted, because
the movement will be visible in group results and needs an agreed explanation.

## 4. Long-term incentive and share-based payment

| Award type | Measurement | Recognition |
|---|---|---|
| Equity-settled, service condition only | Grant-date fair value | Straight-line over the vesting period |
| Equity-settled, non-market performance condition | Grant-date fair value | Over vesting, with the number expected to vest revised each period |
| Equity-settled, market condition | Grant-date fair value, condition modelled in the valuation | Not reversed if the market condition fails, provided service is delivered |
| Cash-settled | Fair value remeasured at each reporting date | Over vesting, with remeasurement through profit |

Grant-date fair values are obtained from the external valuation provider engaged by the Reward
Committee. Finance does not re-model them. Where an award is modified, the incremental fair value is
recognised over the remaining vesting period in addition to the original charge; a modification never
reduces the original charge.

Leavers: awards forfeited for failure of a service condition are reversed. Awards allowed to vest at
the Committee's discretion on a good-leaver determination are treated as a modification, not a
forfeiture, and the charge is accelerated.

## 5. Retention arrangements

Retention awards made outside the normal cycle are approved by the Reward Committee and recorded
against the arrangement reference issued by Group Reward. The current standing arrangement for
critical-role retention is administered under the **Sablewood Retention Pool**, and awards from it
carry the pool reference on every accrual working paper so that the balance can be traced without
naming individuals.

Retention awards are accrued over the retention period, not at the date of grant. Where the award is
contingent on a transaction completing, the charge begins when completion becomes probable, and the
assessment is made by the Chief Financial Officer rather than by the deal team.

## 6. Severance and settlement

A provision for termination benefit is recognised at the earlier of the date the Group can no longer
withdraw the offer, and the date a related restructuring provision is recognised under PG-0024.

An intention to reorganise is not a constructive obligation. A communicated, specific plan naming
affected roles and timing is. Individual settlement agreements under negotiation are provided for
only where the offer is made and cannot be withdrawn.

Payments in lieu of notice, statutory redundancy, enhanced terms, outplacement, legal fees, and any
accelerated share award are all components of the termination cost and are provided for together.
Splitting a settlement across categories to reduce the visible severance figure is prohibited.

## 7. Disclosure interface

Amounts feeding the remuneration report are prepared by Group Reward and reconciled to the ledger by
the Group Financial Controller before the report is drafted. The reconciliation is performed on
aggregate figures. Individual-level detail is exchanged only through the Reward access list.

## 8. Access control

Access to the underlying working papers is granted by the Chief Financial Officer, is reviewed
quarterly, and is withdrawn on role change the same day. Working papers are held in the restricted
Reward workspace and are never attached to a journal in the general ledger; the journal carries the
reference only.
