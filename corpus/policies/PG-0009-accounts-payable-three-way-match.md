# Accounts Payable, Three-Way Match and Payment Runs

This policy governs what Accounts Payable does between an invoice arriving and money leaving the
Group.

## 1. Valid invoice

An invoice is processed only if it shows the supplier's legal name and registration number, an
invoice number, an invoice date, the Group entity being billed, a purchase order reference where
one is required, a description of what was supplied, the tax treatment, and the total due.
Invoices missing any of these are returned to the supplier; they are not corrected by Accounts
Payable on the supplier's behalf.

Invoices are received at the central invoice address or portal. An invoice sent directly to a
budget holder is forwarded unopened to Accounts Payable; it is never entered by the budget holder.

## 2. Three-way match

Every purchase-order-backed invoice is matched to the purchase order and the goods receipt. All
three must agree on supplier, quantity and price.

Where the invoice exceeds the purchase order, the **three-way match tolerance of 2 per cent** of
the order value **or USD 50, whichever is lower** may be released without an amended order. The
tolerance is applied to the order as a whole, never per line, and it is not a permitted overspend
allowance — repeated use with the same supplier is investigated.

Beyond tolerance, the invoice is blocked until the requisitioner obtains an amended purchase order
approved under PG-0007. Accounts Payable does not resolve price disputes; it holds the invoice and
escalates to the requisitioner after five working days and to the Department Head after ten.

## 3. Non-purchase-order invoices

Invoices in the exempt categories listed in PG-0008 §1 are coded and approved by the account owner
under PG-0002. Any other invoice arriving without a purchase order is a policy exception: a
**purchase order is required for any commitment above USD 2,500**, and the invoice is held while a
retrospective order is raised and approved at the higher band required by PG-0008.

## 4. Duplicate prevention

Before posting, every invoice is checked against a **duplicate-invoice review window of 90 days**
on four keys: supplier and invoice number; supplier, amount and invoice date; purchase order and
amount; and bank account and amount. A match on any key stops the invoice for review.

Credit notes are matched to the original invoice before they are applied. An unapplied credit note
older than two close cycles is escalated to the account owner.

## 5. Payment terms and runs

The Group applies **standard payment terms of Net 45** from receipt of a valid invoice, calculated
from the date the invoice is received at the central address, not from the invoice date.

Payment runs are executed twice weekly. Off-cycle payments require Finance Director approval and a
stated reason; supplier pressure alone is not one. Manual payments outside the banking platform
require two authorised signatories and are reported to the Treasurer within one working day.

Payments are never made to a bank account that differs from the verified account on the supplier
master, and never on the basis of details supplied on the invoice itself. Any request to redirect a
payment follows PG-0008 §5 without exception, however urgent it claims to be.

## 6. Supplier statement reconciliation

Statements from the twenty largest suppliers by spend, and from any supplier with a disputed
balance, are reconciled monthly. Differences are cleared before the ledger closes rather than
carried as a reconciling item.

## 7. Close cut-off

**sub-ledgers close on Working Day 5**. Invoices received after the sub-ledger closes are posted in
the following period and, where the goods or service were received before the period end, an
accrual is raised under PG-0015. Accounts Payable provides the accrual listing to each Financial
Controller on Working Day 3.

Goods received but not invoiced at the period end is a standing report reconciled every close; the
balance represents receipts awaiting invoices and is never used as a general provision.

## 8. Metrics

Accounts Payable reports monthly on: percentage matched without intervention, average days to pay,
invoices paid late and why, retrospective purchase orders raised, and duplicate stops released.
These figures go to the Financial Controller and are reviewed at the close meeting.
