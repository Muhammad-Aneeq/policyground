# Corporate Cards and Petty Cash

Corporate cards and petty cash both put Group money directly in an individual's hands. This policy
sets the limits, the reconciliation obligations, and the consequences of not meeting them.

## 1. Eligibility

A corporate card is issued to employees who travel on Group business at least four times a year, or
who hold a purchasing role requiring low-value transactions. Cards are issued in the employee's
name, are not transferable, and may not be used by anyone other than the named holder — including a
manager, an assistant, or a colleague on the same trip.

Applications are approved by the Department Head and the Group Treasurer.

## 2. Limits

The **standard corporate card limit of USD 10,000** per monthly cycle applies to all cardholders.
Higher limits are set as follows:

| Cardholder group | Cycle limit | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | USD 10,000 | Group Treasurer |
| Frequent international traveller | USD 20,000 | Finance Director |
| Executive Committee | USD 25,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Temporary uplift, maximum 60 days | Up to double the standing limit | Finance Director |

A card limit is not spending authority. Every transaction still requires approval under PG-0007 at
its value, and a card may never be used to commit the Group above the cardholder's own band.

## 3. Permitted and prohibited use

Permitted: travel and accommodation within PG-0006 limits, conference registration, low-value
supplies below the purchase order threshold, and emergency operational purchases.

Prohibited: any personal expenditure, however small and however promptly repaid; cash withdrawals
other than a documented travel advance; any commitment requiring a purchase order under PG-0008;
recurring subscriptions, which are contracted centrally; and splitting a purchase across
transactions or cards to stay below a limit.

Personal use is the most common breach and is treated seriously precisely because it looks minor. A
first instance is a documented warning; a second results in card withdrawal.

## 4. Reconciliation

Cardholders code and submit their statement **within 30 days of the expense being incurred**, with
receipts attached. An **itemised receipt is required for any single item above USD 25**, on the same
basis as PG-0006.

Unreconciled transactions older than sixty days result in card suspension until cleared.
Unreconciled amounts older than ninety days are recovered through payroll, after written notice, and
the tax consequence falls to the individual.

Line managers approve their team's card statements monthly and are accountable for what they
approve.

## 5. Lost, stolen or compromised cards

Report to the card provider immediately and to the Group Treasurer within one working day.
Disputed transactions are raised with the provider within the provider's dispute window, which is
typically sixty days; a transaction not disputed in time becomes a Group cost and is investigated.

## 6. Leavers

Cards are cancelled on the last working day. The final statement is reconciled before the final
salary payment is released. The line manager confirms card return as part of the leaver checklist;
an unreturned card is reported to the Treasurer the same week.

## 7. Petty cash

Petty cash floats are held only where a documented operational need exists and no card alternative
is workable. Each float is approved by the Financial Controller, has a named custodian, and is held
in a locked cash box or safe.

- Standard float: USD 2,000. Larger floats require Finance Director approval.
- The **petty cash claim maximum of USD 500** applies per claim; larger amounts go through Accounts
  Payable.
- Every disbursement is supported by a receipt and a signed voucher showing the business purpose.
- The custodian may not approve their own disbursement.
- Floats are counted monthly by a person other than the custodian, and the count is signed and
  retained. Differences of any amount are reported to the Financial Controller; a difference is
  never made good from the custodian's own money, because that conceals the control failure.

Petty cash is never used to pay an employee for services, to make an advance against salary, or to
settle a supplier invoice.
