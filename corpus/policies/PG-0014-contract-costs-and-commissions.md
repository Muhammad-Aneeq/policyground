# Contract Costs and Capitalised Sales Commissions

Costs of winning and delivering a contract are not automatically an expense of the period in which
they are paid. This policy sets out which are capitalised, over what period they are released, and
how the balance is monitored.

## 1. Costs of obtaining a contract

The incremental costs of obtaining a contract — those the Group would not have incurred had the
contract not been won — are capitalised as a contract cost asset where the Group expects to recover
them.

The test is incrementality, not attribution:

| Cost | Treatment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Commission paid only on a signed contract | Capitalise | Incremental |
| Commission payable whether or not the bid succeeds | Expense | Not incremental |
| Bid preparation, travel to pitch, legal review of a proposal | Expense | Incurred regardless of outcome |
| Bonus to a sales manager based on team quota attainment | Assess individually | Incremental only where directly traceable to specific contracts |
| Employer payroll taxes on a capitalised commission | Capitalise | Follows the commission |
| Fixed salary of the salesperson | Expense | Not incremental |

Practical expedient: where the amortisation period would be one year or less, the cost is expensed
as incurred. The Group applies this expedient consistently and discloses that it does so.

## 2. Costs of fulfilling a contract

Fulfilment costs are capitalised only where they relate directly to a contract or an anticipated
contract, they generate or enhance resources used to satisfy performance obligations in the future,
and they are expected to be recovered.

Set-up, mobilisation and design costs incurred before a service begins typically qualify. General
and administrative costs, costs of wasted materials or labour not reflected in the contract price,
and costs relating to obligations already satisfied do not qualify and are expensed as incurred.

Where a cost falls within the scope of another policy — inventory under PG-0029, property, plant
and equipment under PG-0004, intangible assets under PG-0005 — that policy applies first, and this
one does not.

## 3. Amortisation

Contract cost assets are amortised on a systematic basis consistent with the transfer of the goods
or services to which the asset relates. Where the customer is expected to renew, the amortisation
period includes the anticipated renewal period, so the period is often longer than the initial
contract term.

Contract cost assets are **amortised over a period not exceeding 5 years** unless Group Technical
Accounting approves a longer period in writing, supported by evidence of the average customer
relationship life from churn data rather than from a sales forecast.

The amortisation period is reviewed each reporting date. A change in the expected period is a change
in estimate, applied prospectively.

## 4. Impairment

At each reporting date the carrying amount of the contract cost asset is compared with the remaining
consideration the Group expects to receive, less the costs that relate directly to providing the
remaining goods or services. Any excess is impaired immediately.

Impairment is assessed before testing the wider cash-generating unit under PG-0030, so that a
contract-specific loss is not absorbed into a larger unit and hidden. Impairment losses on contract
cost assets are not reversed.

## 5. Presentation

Contract cost assets are presented separately from contract assets arising from performance.
Amortisation is presented in the same line as the related revenue's cost — commission amortisation
in selling expense, fulfilment cost amortisation in cost of sales.

## 6. Controls

- The commission plan is provided to Finance before the start of each plan year, and any mid-year
  change is notified within five working days, because a change in plan design can change the
  incrementality conclusion.
- The capitalised commission balance is reconciled monthly to the underlying contract population.
- A quarterly report of contracts terminated early identifies the associated unamortised balance and
  triggers immediate write-off of the balance relating to terminated contracts.
- Any capitalised balance relating to a customer in dispute is reviewed with the credit team under
  PG-0028.
