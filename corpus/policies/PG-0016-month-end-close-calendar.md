# Month-End Close Calendar and Responsibilities

The close is a fixed timetable, not a best-effort exercise. This policy states what happens on each
working day, who owns it, and what happens when a deadline is missed.

## 1. The calendar

Working Day 1 is the first working day of the month following the period being closed.

| Working Day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| WD 1 | Bank statements loaded and posted; cash reconciled | Treasury |
| WD 2 | Payroll accrual issued to entities; fixed asset depreciation run | Payroll; Fixed Assets |
| WD 3 | Accounts Payable accrual listing issued to entities | Shared Services |
| WD 4 | Revenue cut-off review; intercompany balances agreed | Revenue; Entity Controllers |
| WD 5 | Sub-ledgers closed; accruals and prepayments posted | Entity Controllers |
| WD 6 | Balance sheet reconciliations completed and reviewed | Entity Controllers |
| WD 7 | Flux analysis prepared and reviewed with budget holders | Entity Controllers |
| WD 8 | Group submission; consolidation opens | Entity Controllers; Group Reporting |
| WD 9 | Consolidation, elimination, translation | Group Reporting |
| WD 10 | Group review meeting; management pack issued | Financial Controller |

Two anchors govern everything else: **sub-ledgers close on Working Day 5** and **group submission on
Working Day 8**. Every other task is scheduled so that these two dates hold.

## 2. Sub-ledger close

At Working Day 5 the payables, receivables, fixed asset, inventory and payroll sub-ledgers are
closed to posting. Any entry required after that point is a general ledger journal under PG-0017,
approved at the level appropriate to its value, and it is listed on the late-entry log.

Entities post accruals and prepayments before the sub-ledger close, applying the **accrual threshold
of USD 2,500** from PG-0015.

## 3. Flux analysis

Before submission, each entity explains movements against both the prior period and budget. An
explanation is required where a line moves by more than 10 per cent **and** more than USD 100,000.

An explanation names the driver. "Higher costs" is not an explanation; "two contractors extended
through August, USD 140,000, agreed with the Head of Engineering" is. Explanations that repeat the
prior month's wording are rejected by Group Reporting and returned.

## 4. Submission

Submission at Working Day 8 includes the trial balance, the reconciliation status report, the flux
commentary, the intercompany confirmation, and the sign-off from the entity's Financial Controller.

A submission is incomplete if any of these is missing. Incomplete submissions are logged and
reported to the Finance Director; a second incomplete submission in a rolling quarter triggers a
review of the entity's close process.

## 5. Late entries and reopening

The ledger is not reopened after submission. Where an error is found between submission and the
group review meeting, it is recorded as a top-side adjustment at group level with the entity
notified, and the entity corrects its own ledger in the following period.

Errors found after the group review meeting are assessed against materiality under PG-0001.
Immaterial errors are corrected in the following period and recorded on the summary of uncorrected
misstatements. Material errors are escalated to the Finance Director the same day.

## 6. Quarter and year end

At quarter end the calendar extends by two working days to accommodate tax, disclosure and review
procedures. At year end it extends by five. The extended calendar is published in the finance
calendar each September for the following year, so that leave and audit fieldwork can be planned
around it.

## 7. Close metrics

Group Reporting publishes each month: submissions on time, reconciliations completed by Working Day
6, late entries by entity and value, top-side adjustments raised, and the number of flux
explanations returned. These are reviewed at the Financial Controllers' forum, and a persistent
outlier is given a documented improvement plan rather than a reminder.
