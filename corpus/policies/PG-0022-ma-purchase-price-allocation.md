# Business Combinations — Purchase Price Allocation and Deal Costs

**Distribution:** Chief Financial Officer, Finance Director, Group Technical Accounting, and the
corporate development team members named on the transaction access list. Circulation of this policy
alongside a live transaction reference is itself market-sensitive information and is subject to the
Group insider list procedure.

## 1. Why this policy is restricted

The existence of a transaction is price-sensitive before announcement. Working papers prepared under
this policy name targets, prices and expected synergies. The accounting method is ordinary; the
association between a target and a valuation is not.

## 2. Identifying the acquirer and the acquisition date

The acquirer is the entity obtaining control, which is not always the entity issuing consideration.
The acquisition date is when control passes, which is not necessarily completion under the sale
agreement — conditions precedent, regulatory clearance and effective-date clauses are assessed
individually.

Where the assessment is finely balanced, Group Technical Accounting documents both readings and the
reason for the conclusion before the first accounting entry is made, not afterwards.

## 3. Consideration transferred

Includes cash, the fair value of equity issued, the fair value of contingent consideration, and the
settlement of any pre-existing relationship at fair value.

Excludes: payments to selling shareholders that are remuneration for post-combination service, which
are a compensation cost of the combined group; the reimbursement of the seller's transaction costs;
and any amount contingent on continued employment, which is remuneration however the sale agreement
labels it.

The employment-linked test is applied strictly. Where an earn-out is forfeited if a selling
shareholder leaves, it is remuneration. This conclusion is documented before signing wherever
possible, because it materially changes reported post-deal earnings and is difficult to explain if
reached late.

## 4. Contingent consideration

Classified as a liability or as equity at the acquisition date. A liability is remeasured to fair
value at each reporting date through profit or loss; an equity-classified arrangement is not
remeasured. Reclassification after the acquisition date is not permitted.

Remeasurement movements are presented separately from operating results and are explained in the
flux commentary. Where an earn-out liability falls because the target is underperforming, the
resulting credit and the underlying underperformance are reported together, never as a standalone
gain.

## 5. Identifiable assets and liabilities

Recognised at fair value at the acquisition date, whether or not they were recognised in the
target's own books. In practice this most often surfaces customer relationships, order backlog,
brands, developed technology, favourable and unfavourable contract terms, and contingent
liabilities that the target had disclosed but not provided for.

Not recognised separately: assembled workforce, expected synergies, and any restructuring the
acquirer intends but is not obliged to perform at the acquisition date. Those fall into goodwill or,
for the restructuring, are accounted for post-combination under PG-0024.

External valuation support is obtained for any intangible expected to exceed USD 5,000,000 in fair
value, and for every brand and technology asset regardless of value.

## 6. Measurement period

Provisional amounts may be adjusted for up to twelve months from the acquisition date, but only for
facts and circumstances existing at that date. A change in estimate arising from post-acquisition
events is not a measurement-period adjustment and goes through profit or loss.

The distinction is policed by Group Technical Accounting. A measurement-period adjustment that
conveniently reduces post-deal amortisation is examined for whether the fact genuinely existed at
acquisition.

## 7. Deal costs

Advisory, legal, due diligence, valuation and broker fees are expensed as incurred and are presented
separately from underlying operating costs. They are never capitalised into goodwill.

Costs of issuing debt are deducted from the carrying amount of the debt. Costs of issuing equity are
deducted from equity. Where a single adviser fee covers more than one of these, it is allocated on a
reasonable basis and the allocation is documented.

Live transactions are given a codename at the point an external adviser is engaged, and all
invoices, purchase orders and working papers use it. The codename currently in use for the
transaction on the corporate development register is **Project Harrowgate**; suppliers are onboarded
against the codename and never against the target's name.

## 8. Post-completion

Opening balance sheets are integrated into the group consolidation at the first close after
completion. The target's accounting policies are aligned to this manual before its first submission,
with differences quantified and reported to the Finance Director. Alignment is not deferred to the
following year end.
