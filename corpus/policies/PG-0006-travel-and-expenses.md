# Travel, Entertainment and Employee Expenses

This policy sets the limits and the evidence standard for employee-incurred cost. It applies to
every employee, contractor and director of the Group, and to any expense recharged to the Group by
a third party on an employee's behalf.

## 1. Principles

- Spend Group money as you would spend your own, on business that the Group would recognise as
  necessary.
- The cheapest reasonable option is the default. Where it is not chosen, the reason is recorded on
  the claim.
- Nobody approves their own expense, and nobody approves the expense of a person who approves
  theirs.

## 2. Submission and evidence

Claims are submitted **within 30 days of the expense being incurred**. Claims submitted later are
paid only with Finance Director approval and, where a tax deadline has passed, may be treated as
taxable to the individual.

An **itemised receipt is required for any single item above USD 25**. An itemised receipt shows the
line items, not just the card total; a card slip alone is not sufficient for meals or hotels. Where
a receipt is genuinely unobtainable, a missing-receipt declaration countersigned by the approver is
accepted no more than twice per person per year.

## 3. Air and rail travel

| Journey | Class |
|---|---|
| Rail, any duration | Standard class; first class only where cheaper than the standard fare booked on the day |
| Flights under 6 hours | Economy |
| Flights of 6 hours or more | Premium economy |
| Flights of 6 hours or more, Executive Committee members | Business |

Travel is booked through the Group travel provider at least 14 days in advance where the trip is
foreseeable. Bookings made outside the provider or inside the 14-day window require the reason on
the claim. Personal travel may be combined with business travel only where it creates no
additional cost to the Group; the employee pays any incremental fare directly.

Airline loyalty benefits earned on Group travel may be retained by the employee. They may never
influence the choice of carrier where a cheaper reasonable option exists.

## 4. Accommodation

Hotel bookings are made through the Group travel provider wherever the provider has coverage. The
**standard nightly room rate cap of USD 250** applies to all locations other than those on the
Tier 1 list. For locations on that list — currently New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo,
Singapore and Sydney — the **Tier 1 city nightly room rate cap of USD 350** applies. Caps are
exclusive of local taxes and are per night, not averaged across a trip.

Where a conference rate exceeds the cap, the conference rate may be used if booking outside the
event would cost more in total once travel time and transfers are considered.

## 5. Meals

Employees claim either actual cost or the per diem, not both, and the choice is made per trip.

- **domestic meal per diem of USD 75** per full day
- **international meal per diem of USD 110** per full day

A day on which a meal is provided by a host, a conference or an airline is reduced by one third per
provided meal. Alcohol is not reimbursable other than as part of a client entertainment claim under
§7. Actual-cost meal claims for a group are made by the most senior person present.

## 6. Ground transport and mileage

Public transport is the default. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable for journeys with luggage,
outside normal hours, or where no reasonable public option exists.

Where an employee uses their own vehicle, the **mileage reimbursement rate of USD 0.67 per mile**
applies and covers fuel, wear and insurance. Mileage claims state the start point, destination and
business purpose. Commuting between home and a normal place of work is never reimbursable.

Vehicle hire is limited to intermediate class. Fuel is reimbursed on receipt; prepaid fuel options
are not reimbursable.

## 7. Client entertainment and gifts

Client entertainment is reimbursable where a business purpose and the names of those attending are
recorded on the claim. Entertainment where no client is present is a staff event and is approved
under §8 instead.

Gifts to a client of more than USD 250 in value require Finance Director approval before they are
given. No gift, entertainment, or hospitality of any value may be offered to a public official,
regardless of local custom, and any request for one is reported to the Group Compliance Officer.

## 8. Staff events and recognition

Team events require Department Head approval in advance and are capped at USD 120 per attendee per
event, and at four events per team per year. Individual recognition awards above USD 100 are made
through the payroll recognition scheme so that tax is handled correctly, and never as an expense
reimbursement.

## 9. Non-reimbursable items

Traffic and parking fines; personal grooming; in-room entertainment; lost personal property; travel
insurance purchased individually where Group cover exists; membership of clubs; political
donations; and any expenditure that would require a purchase order under PG-0008. A **purchase
order is required for any commitment above USD 2,500**, and an expense claim is never a route
around that requirement.

## 10. Approval routing

Claims route to the claimant's line manager, and additionally to the Finance Director where the
claim total exceeds USD 5,000 or contains any item flagged under §9. Approvers are accountable for
what they approve; approving without reviewing the underlying receipts is a control failure and is
reported as such.
