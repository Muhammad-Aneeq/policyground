# Balance Sheet Reconciliation Standard

A reconciliation is a statement that the balance is real and explainable. Ticking a box that a
schedule exists is not a reconciliation. This policy defines the standard, the frequency and the
review.

## 1. Scope

Every general ledger account with a balance, and every account with nil balance that had movement in
the period. Accounts are assigned a risk rating that drives frequency:

| Risk | Examples | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| High | Cash, receivables, payables, intercompany, revenue-related contract balances, provisions, suspense | Monthly |
| Medium | Prepayments, accruals, fixed assets, inventory, payroll balance sheet accounts | Monthly |
| Low | Share capital, long-term borrowings with no movement, dormant balances | Quarterly |

Any account with a suspense character is high risk regardless of size.

## 2. What a reconciliation contains

1. The general ledger balance, taken from the ledger, not retyped.
2. The supporting balance, from the sub-ledger, bank statement, third-party confirmation or
   schedule.
3. Every reconciling item, each with a description, an amount, the date it arose, and the named
   owner responsible for clearing it.
4. An explicit conclusion signed by the preparer.
5. Evidence of independent review.

An unexplained difference is a reconciling item with the description "unexplained". It is never
absorbed into a balancing figure, and never written off to profit without approval under PG-0017.

## 3. Ageing of reconciling items

| Age | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Current close | Normal |
| 2 closes | Owner states the clearance plan on the reconciliation |
| 3 closes | Reported to the entity Financial Controller |
| Over 3 closes | Reported to the Group Financial Controller with a written clearance plan |

Ageing is measured from when the item arose, not from when it was noticed.

## 4. Thresholds for escalation

An individual reconciling item at or above the **clearly trivial threshold of USD 75,000** is
reported in the close file with a named owner and a clearance date.

The aggregate of unexplained differences across an entity is measured against the **group
performance materiality of USD 1,500,000**; where the aggregate exceeds 10 per cent of that amount,
the Finance Director is notified before submission rather than after.

The **intercompany mismatch tolerance of USD 1,000** applies to intercompany reconciling items, per
PG-0019. Differences within tolerance are cleared to the designated difference account by the
selling entity; differences above tolerance are resolved between the entities before submission.

## 5. Review

Review is performed by someone other than the preparer, with the standing and knowledge to
challenge. The reviewer checks that the supporting balance is genuinely independent of the ledger,
that reconciling items are explained rather than merely listed, that old items have a plan, and that
the conclusion is supported.

Review is evidenced with a name and date. A review performed after group submission is not a review;
reconciliations are completed by Working Day 6 under PG-0016 for that reason.

## 6. Cash

Bank reconciliations are prepared from the bank statement directly, never from a treasury system
extract alone. Unpresented items older than six months are investigated and, where the payment is
confirmed stale, cancelled with the payee notified. Deposits in transit older than five working days
are investigated the same week.

Every bank account in the Group appears on the treasury account register. An account not on the
register is reported to the Treasurer immediately; there is no legitimate reason for one to exist.

## 7. Reporting

Group Reporting publishes a monthly reconciliation status by entity: completed on time, completed
late, not completed, items aged over three closes, and total unexplained difference. Entities with
repeat exceptions are placed on a documented improvement plan with monthly progress review.
